# Rules and Regulations

## Intercollegiate Quiz Competition

### (UG Science Students – Basic Science)

## 1. Eligibility

- The quiz competition is open to Undergraduate Science students from recognized colleges.
- Only students from Basic Science disciplines such as: Microbiology, Biotechnology, Biochemistry, Botany, Zoology, Chemistry, Physics, and related subjects are eligible.
- Participants must carry a valid College ID card on the day of the event.

## 2. Team Composition

- Each participating college may send a maximum of one team (or as specified).
- Each team must consist of 2 members.
- Team members must be from the same institution.
- No change of team members is allowed after registration.

## 3. Registration Rules

- Teams must complete registration before the deadline.
- Spot registrations may be allowed only if seats are available.
- Registered teams must report at least 30 minutes before the competition begins.

## 4. Quiz Format

The quiz will consist of multiple rounds such as:

1. Preliminary Round (Written/MCQ)
2. Buzzer Round
3. Rapid Fire Round
4. Visual/Scientific Identification Round
5. Final Round

_(The number and type of rounds may vary depending on the organizers.)_

## 5. Topics Covered

Questions will be based on Basic Science at UG level, including:

- General Science Awareness
- Microbiology & Biotechnology
- Botany & Zoology
- Chemistry & Physics
- Environmental Science
- Scientific Discoveries and Current Trends

## 6. Time Limit

- Each team will be given 10–30 seconds to answer a question depending on the round.
- Rapid fire responses must be given within the allotted time without interruption.

## 7. Scoring System

- Marks will be awarded for each correct answer.
- Negative marking may be applied for incorrect answers in certain rounds.
- No marks will be awarded for unanswered questions.

## 8. Use of Electronic Devices

- Mobile phones, smart watches, calculators, or any electronic devices are strictly prohibited.
- Any team found using unfair means will be disqualified immediately.

## 9. Discipline and Conduct

- Participants must maintain decorum throughout the event.
- Any form of misbehavior, misconduct, or disturbance will lead to disqualification.
- Teams are expected to respect the quizmaster, judges, and fellow participants.

## 10. Buzzer Rules (if applicable)

- Teams must press the buzzer only after the question is fully read.
- Early buzzing may result in penalty or loss of opportunity.

## 11. Tie-Breaker

- In case of a tie, a tie-breaker round will be conducted.
- The decision of the quizmaster and judges will be final.

## 12. Judges' Decision

- The decision of the organizing committee and judges is final and binding.
- No objections will be entertained after the declaration of results.

## 13. Certificates and Prizes

- Certificates will be provided to all participants.
- Winners and runners-up will receive trophies/prizes as announced during the fest.

## 14. Organizer's Rights

- The organizers reserve the right to modify the rules or format of the quiz at any time.
- The schedule may change depending on event requirements.

---

**Note:** All participants are requested to arrive on time and cooperate for the smooth conduct of the competition.
