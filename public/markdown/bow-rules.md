# SCIENTIFIC BEST OUT OF WASTE EVENT

**Bring creativity, not ready-made items! Rules apply strictly.**

## 1. Eligibility

- The competition is open to all PU and undergraduate students from recognized colleges/universities.
- Participants must carry a valid college ID card on the event day.
- Participation can be individual or team-based (maximum 2–3 members per team)

## 2. Theme

- The theme for the event is Scientific "Best out of Waste".
- **Theme line:** "Creation of a scientific model / concept / application using waste or discarded materials."
- The product must be prepared only from waste / discarded materials.
- Limited use of supporting materials like glue, thread, tape, or color is allowed.

## 3. Registration

- Registration is mandatory for all participants.
- On-spot registrations will be allowed
- The registration fee is 100 rupees (non-refundable)

## 4. Time Limit

- Participants will be given 90–120 minutes to complete their product.
- Extra time will not be permitted under any circumstances.
- Late submission will lead to marks deduction / disqualification.

## 5. Materials

- Participants must bring their own waste materials.
- Materials such as plastic bottles, cardboard, newspapers, old CDs, cloth, etc. are permitted.
- Ready-made items or pre-assembled models, sharp objects, and hazardous materials are strictly prohibited.

## Terms & Conditions

- The product must be created on the spot during the allotted time.
- Any form of plagiarism or pre-prepared work will lead to immediate disqualification.
- Participants are responsible for the safety of their materials.
- The organizing committee is not responsible for loss or damage of personal belongings.
- The decision of the judging panel will be final and binding.
- Misbehavior, violation of rules, or damage to venue property will result in disqualification.
- Participants are responsible for their own safety during the event.
- Participants must maintain cleanliness at their workstations.
- Participants must cooperate with volunteers and organizers.
- The organizing committee reserves the right to modify rules or timing if required.
- Participation in the event implies acceptance of all rules and regulations.

## List of Waste Materials Allowed

### 1. Paper & Cardboard Waste (Highly Recommended)

- Old newspapers
- Used notebooks
- Cardboard boxes
- Paper cups, paper plates

### 2. Plastic Waste (Clean & Safe Only)

_(Must be washed and dried)_

- Plastic bottles (water, soft drink, IV fluid bottles – empty)
- Bottle caps
- Plastic containers
- Pens (ink removed)
- Disposable spoons, cups
- Plastic straws

### 3. Metal Waste (Safe & Non-Sharp)

- Old keys
- Bottle lids (metal)
- Aluminum foil (used & cleaned)
- Old nuts, bolts, screws

### 4. Electronic & Electrical Waste (Low Power Only)

- Old CDs/DVDs
- Discarded wires (without power supply)
- Broken calculators
- Old circuit boards (no live current)
- LED bulbs (non-working)

### 5. Textile & Rubber Waste

- Old clothes / fabric pieces
- Ribbons
- Wool threads
- Old gloves (non-medical)
- Rubber bands

### 6. Natural Waste (Eco-Science Focus)

- Dry leaves
- Coconut shells
- Seeds & husks
- Sawdust
- Egg shells

### Permitted Supporting Materials (Limited Use)

_(Not considered waste but allowed for assembly)_

- Glue / Fevicol
- Thread
- Tape
- Pins
- Poster colors (minimum)

### Strictly Not Allowed

- Thermocol
- Live electrical connections
- Chemicals / acids / reagents
- Sharp objects (blades, needles)
- Fire, candles, crackers
- Fresh purchased decorative items

## Approved Scientific Topics

**Note:** Participants must select a topic related to core scientific concepts, principles, systems, or applications.

### Physics

- Simple machines (lever, pulley, inclined plane)
- Laws of motion
- Renewable energy (solar, wind, hydro)
- Reflection and refraction of light
- Electricity and circuits (non-live)
- Magnetism and electromagnetic principles
- Pressure in liquids and gases
- Optics related

### Chemistry

- Water purification and filtration
- Acid–base and pH scale (conceptual model)
- Separation techniques
- Periodic table concept models
- Corrosion and prevention
- Green chemistry concepts
- Chemical bonding (ball-and-stick models)

### Biology (Human & General)

- Human respiratory system
- Human circulatory system
- Digestive system
- Nervous system
- Cell structure (plant / animal cell)
- Photosynthesis and respiration
- Life cycles

### Microbiology

- Structure of bacterial cell
- Virus structure (conceptual)
- Modes of disease transmission
- Sterilization and disinfection methods
- Culture techniques (model form only)
- Normal flora and pathogens
- Hospital infection control

### Biochemistry

- DNA double helix
- RNA structure
- Enzyme action (lock & key model)
- Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids classification
- Metabolic pathways (overview models)
- Biomolecules and their functions

### Mathematics

- 3D geometrical shapes
- Pythagoras theorem
- Coordinate geometry
- Probability models
- Mathematical puzzles with logic
- Statistics and data representation
- Symmetry and patterns

### Botany

- Parts of a plant
- Transport in plants
- Photosynthesis mechanism
- Plant cell structure
- Types of roots and stems
- Seed germination
- Economic importance of plants

### Zoology

- Animal cell structure
- Human skeleton system
- Digestive system in animals
- Food chain and food web
- Animal classification
- Adaptations in animals
- Sense organs

### Environmental / Interdisciplinary Science

- Waste segregation and recycling
- Biomedical waste management
- Rainwater harvesting
- Water conservation
- Ecosystem and biodiversity
- Climate change (conceptual models)
- Sustainable development

---

## Important Notes

- In addition to the approved list of topics, participants may also present any other model, provided it is strictly related to the field of science and follows a clear scientific approach, principle, or concept.
- The model must be prepared exclusively using waste or discarded materials.
- Purely decorative or newly purchased items are not permitted.
